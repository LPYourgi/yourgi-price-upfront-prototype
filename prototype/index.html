What this prototype is proposing

Four annotated points. Toggle the pins off with the Annotations switch to see the clean screens.
  • 1
    Fee-inclusive price on the browse list (recommended earliest point). Every pro card shows the true per-walk price — base + 5% fee — so parents compare real cost while choosing, before entering any flow. This is the earliest placement where a price is meaningful.
  • 2
    Fee-inclusive price at the booking widget. Matches the current Figma handoff — footer above “Request to Book” on mobile, beside the Book button on desktop — now showing the fee-inclusive number.
  • 3
    Estimate gets more accurate as details are chosen. total = base × walks × 1.05. Picking the number of walks turns the per-walk price into a real total, which is the “more accurate estimate” the ticket asks for.
  • ?
    Open decision: is the early number binding or an estimate that can change at checkout? The copy here says “includes 5% service fee” and stops short of promising the final charge — that call is still needed (see project-context.md, Open Question 3).
